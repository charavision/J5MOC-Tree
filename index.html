J5MOC - LEGO Johnny 5 MOC

J5MOC is a fan-made LEGO Johnny 5 MOC inspired by the movie Short Circuit. This website includes the interactive 3D viewer for the LEGO Johnny Five model, project updates, community links, building information and related content.
